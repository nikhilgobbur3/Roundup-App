# Episode 1: App Walkthrough

## Duration
~60 seconds

## Screen Recording Tips
- Use a phone screen recorder (built-in Android/iOS screen record)
- Clean the phone display before recording
- Landscape or portrait — portrait recommended for mobile demos
- Tap slowly so viewers can follow the flow
- Add a subtle touch indicator in editing if possible

---

## Script

### 0:00 – 0:08 | Intro

**Screen Recording:**
App logo centered on screen. Animated tagline fades in below: "Round up your expenses, effortlessly."

**Script:**
"Why do most of us never save? Because saving never happens by itself. RoundUp fixes that — a UPI expense tracker that rounds up every payment to the nearest ₹10 and saves the spare change for you. No manual entries, no complicated setups."

### 0:08 – 0:18 | Signup

**Screen Recording:**
Show Sign Up screen. Type name, email, password. Tap "Create Account". Brief loading spinner → lands on home screen.

**Screen Recording:**
Home screen shows the "Your Wealth" hero: Savings ₹0.00 in big letters. Empty transaction list. Swipe or scroll slightly.

**Script:**
"You start with a clean slate. Every expense you track gets rounded up to the nearest ₹10 — the spare change goes straight into savings."

---

### 0:25 – 0:33 | QR Scan

**Screen Recording:**
Tap the QR icon in top-right corner. Camera opens (or show manual entry screen for web/simulator). Point at a UPI QR code — or type a UPI ID manually like "test@okicici".

**Script:**
"Scan any UPI QR code — or type a UPI ID if you don't have a QR handy. RoundUp works both ways."

---

### 0:33 – 0:42 | Amount + Roundup Preview

**Screen Recording:**
Enter amount ₹153. The roundup preview appears below: "You'll pay ₹160.00" and "Spare change saved +₹7.00".

**Script:**
"Enter the amount you paid. RoundUp instantly shows you the math — ₹153 becomes ₹160, and ₹7 goes to your savings. No calculators needed."

### 0:42 – 0:48 | Pay → Celebration

**Screen Recording:**
Tap "Pay ₹153.00" (the exact bill). Brief loading spinner (1.5s). **Celebration screen**: gold confetti rains down, a gold checkmark pops in with a haptic buzz, and the pill "+₹7.00 invested in savings" drops in below "₹153.00 paid to Blue Tokai".

**Script:**
"Tap Pay — the exact bill amount. Then the app celebrates with you: confetti, a buzz, and your spare change now invested. That moment of delight is what turns users into repeat users."

**Note:** Currently in dummy payment mode for testing. The transaction is recorded to the backend — savings and transaction history all update for real. When ready, one config flag activates the full Razorpay checkout with real payment methods.

### 0:48 – 0:55 | Home Screen Updated

**Screen Recording:**
Navigate back to home. The "Your Wealth" number jumped up. Transaction appears in the list below.

**Script:**
"Expense recorded, spare change saved. Your wealth just grew — and it updates in real time."

### 0:55 – 1:00 | Profile + Tech Stack + Outro

**Screen Recording:**
Show Profile screen with user details. Overlay text fades in showing the tech stack: "React Native · Expo · Spring Boot · Razorpay · Railway · Neon"

**Script:**

**Outro:**
"RoundUp — round up your expenses, effortlessly. Follow for more."

## Editing Notes
- Add subtle background music (royalty-free, low volume)
- Use smooth crossfade transitions between clips
- Text overlays should match the fintech theme (blue/green palette)
- Keep captions minimal — let the screen recording do the talking
- End card should stay on screen for 3-4 seconds with the LinkedIn/GitHub links
